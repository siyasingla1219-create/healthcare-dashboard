Key findings & responsible use

Records are spread almost evenly across conditions, test results, medications, insurers and admission types. Charts show patterns, not cause and effect. The dataset looks randomly generated, so it cannot represent real hospital behaviour; real patient data needs consent, privacy and access controls.
